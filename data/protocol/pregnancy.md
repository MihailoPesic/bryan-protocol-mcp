# Pregnancy

## **Pregnancy**

**If pregnant and on the stack.. What do you do:**

1. The ingredients in the following Blueprint products are known to be safe during pregnancy. However, we suggest you consult your doctor before use if pregnant or breastfeeding:

1. Longevity Protein
2. Blueberry Nut Mix
3. EVOO
4. Protein powders
5. Collagen

2. The following supplements included in the Blueprint stack should be **avoided** during pregnancy:

1. Longevity Mix, Ashwagandha, Curcumin, Red Yeast Rice, Nicotinamide Riboside, CA-AKG, Glucosamine Sulfate, Creatine, Sodium Hyaluronate, N-Acetyl-Cysteine.

3. Consider **adding** the following supplements:

1. Prenatal Vitamin with adequate methylated B vitamins (at least 600 mcg Methylfolate, 2.5 mcg methylcobalamin).
2. Magnesium 500-600 mg daily.
3. Iron 30 mg daily.
4. Calcium 1200 mg daily.
5. Omega 3 Fatty Acids DHA/EPA 1000-2000 mg daily (Low metal source, Blueprint DHA/EPA out in Q1 2025) .

4. Nutritional recommendations:

1. Balanced diet with at least 100 g of protein daily.
2. Avoid processed sugars and foods to reduce gestational diabetes risk.
3. Consume at least 30 g of fiber daily.
4. Avoid raw meat, processed meat, unpasteurized cheeses, and high mercury fish.
5. Avoid alcohol and limit caffeine to 200 mg daily.

5. Exercise Recommendations:

1. Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity exercise per week.
2. Strength training at least 90 minutes per week.
3. Consider adding in pelvic floor exercises.
