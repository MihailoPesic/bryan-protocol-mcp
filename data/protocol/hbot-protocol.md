## **HBOT Protocol**

**Protocol**

**Conditions: **100% oxygen at 2 atmospheric pressures.

**Schedule: **60 sessions, 5 sessions a week, duration: 90 min (20 min followed by 5 min air break, repeat)

**Chamber:  **[HPO Tech](https://www.hpotech.com)  hard shell hyperbaric chamber, ambient pressurized air (normal 21% Oxygen) with 100% oxygen delivered through a face-mask.

**Caution: avoid chambers with 100% ambient oxygen: **

- irritates the eyes and causes cataracts,
- higher ignition risk
- harder to take a break or emergency-stop the session
