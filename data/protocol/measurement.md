# Measurement

## **Measurement**

We are working on a Mobile App & Biomarker testing products to enable you to track your health, measure blood work, engage with others, share updates, and meetup in real life.

**Routine Measurement **

1. [Blood draw, every 3-6 months](https://blueprintbiomarkers.com)
2. Full body MRI annually (if over 40 or a family history of high risk)
3. Mole check, annually
4. Dentist, every 6 months
5. Eye doctor, annually
6. Blood glucose CPM
7. Breast self-examination, monthly (ideally a few days after your period ends)
8. Testicular self-examination, monthly
